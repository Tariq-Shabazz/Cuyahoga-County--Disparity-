# Cuyahoga County Disparity Dashboard

**Research & Analysis by Tariq Shabazz M.P.P.**

An interactive dashboard analyzing the 2020 Griffin & Strong P.C. and 2025 Keen Independent Research disparity studies for Cuyahoga County, Ohio. Includes the **Green Line Project** — a lineage-based disparity remediation framework and local atonement measure inspired by Dr. William A. Darity Jr. & A. Kirsten Mullen's *From Here to Equality* (UNC Press, 2020).

## Green Line Project

The Green Line Project is a targeted contracting equity framework introducing a **dual-pillar lineage-based** approach:

1. **Pillar I — ADOS Lineage**: American-Descended Business Enterprise (ABE) certification for descendants of U.S. chattel slavery. Inspired by the eligibility framework in *From Here to Equality* by Dr. William A. Darity Jr. & A. Kirsten Mullen.
2. **Pillar II — Redline Geography**: Redlined Community Business (RCB) tier for firms rooted in HOLC-graded C/D census tracts — a race-neutral supplemental mechanism.

> *"Reparations are a program of acknowledgment, redress, and closure for a grievous injustice."*
> — Darity & Mullen, From Here to Equality (2020)
